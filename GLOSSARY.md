# Tournament play

## Language

**Playoff**:
The elimination stage following completed groups, including the main draw and optional consolation draw.
_Avoid_: group stage bracket

**Advancer**:
A participant selected for a playoff draw by group placement or wildcard rules.

**Reseeding**:
Reconciliation of playoff entrants with current group standings. Results that depend on changed entrants are invalidated.

**History correction**:
An edit to a recorded result together with its consequences for dependent matches or later rounds.

**Tournament evaluation**:
Derivation of current progress, ranked results and winner from recorded matches. Completion time records when completion was saved, not when results were viewed.
